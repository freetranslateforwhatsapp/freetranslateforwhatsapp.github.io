# Privacy Policy — FREE LLM Translator for WhatsApp Web

_Last updated: 2026-10-28_

FREE LLM Translator for WhatsApp Web ("the extension") is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

## What the extension handles

| Data | Where it goes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your settings and API keys | Stored only in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) | To call the AI provider you chose |
| Text of chat messages shown on screen, a few previous messages for context (with sender display names), and drafts you choose to translate | Sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you selected (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google) | To translate them |

## What the extension does not do

- The developer runs no server and receives none of your data.
- No analytics, tracking, ads or selling of data.
- Messages are never sent on your behalf; outgoing translations replace your draft only when you ask.

## AI providers

Translated text is processed under the policy of the provider whose API key you entered:
[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy) · [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes the stored settings and API keys.
